You are an engineer. You don't "tinker" endlessly; you deploy systems. This 21-day boot camp is designed to build your Manufacturing Stack. By Day 21, you will not just know how to use your tools—you will have a workflow that turns code into physical objects in minutes.

CRITICAL SAFETY PROTOCOL: Do NOT attempt to laser cut Mirror Acrylic with your Falcon 2 (Diode Laser). The blue light passes through the clear layer, reflects off the mirror backing, and can destroy your laser module. We will use the "Faux Mirror" Hybrid Method (Wood/Black Acrylic + Mirror Vinyl) exclusively.

Day 13: The Code (Python)

Do not draw the map manually. Use the `osmnx` library to fetch water polygons and `shapely` to generate depth rings.

import osmnx as ox from shapely.geometry import Polygon # 1. Fetch Water Data place = "Blakeslee, Pennsylvania, USA" water = ox.geometries_from_place(place, tags={'natural': 'water'}) # 2. Create Depth Rings (Buffer) # Negative buffer simulates depth deep_water = water.buffer(-0.001) abyss = water.buffer(-0.002) # 3. Export to DXF with ezdxf...

Days 14-18: The Fabrication

  • Layer 1 (Deep): Laser cut Solid Blue Acrylic.
  • Layer 2 (Shallow): Laser cut Light Blue Acrylic (Ring).
  • Layer 3 (Land): Laser cut Walnut Plywood (Stained). Apply 3M adhesive to back BEFORE cutting.
  • Layer 4 (Data): Cricut Cut Roads/Text from Gold Mirror Vinyl.
  • Frame: 3D Print "Click-Lock" corners in Matte Black PLA.

Days 19-21: Assembly

Peel and stick. The 3M adhesive turns the wood layer into a giant sticker. Apply Gold Vinyl to the Wood. Snap the sandwich into the 3D printed corners. Hang it on the wall.
